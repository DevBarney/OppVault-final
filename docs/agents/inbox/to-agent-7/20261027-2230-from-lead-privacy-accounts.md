# Privacy notice and terms need a short section on accounts

Visitors can now create an account to save opportunities (D-009, `docs/ACCOUNTS-SETUP.md`). Please update `/privacy` (and `/terms` if it fits) in plain, honest language:

- What we keep: email address, optional name, which sign-in method they used (email, Google, LinkedIn or X), and their list of saved opportunities.
- Why: so they can save opportunities and find them again. We do not use it for anything else.
- Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider (Supabase) and are never visible to us.
- They can delete their account and saved list themselves, any time, from My saved.
- Cookies: two strictly necessary sign-in cookies, only set after sign-in.

Do not claim anything the code does not do. Ask me if unsure.
